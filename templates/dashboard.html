Data Coverage

Scope of the analytical model

Important data note

The source dataset is synthetic and publicly published for FP&A research and analytical use. It is not a representation of an actual company's financial performance.

The model intentionally retains transparent data-quality indicators rather than presenting the dataset as perfectly complete.

Methodology & Data Story

How the source data was transformed into the management reporting and planning outputs.

End-to-end analytical workflow
FP&A

From accounting postings to management insight

The project combines SQL, Python, Excel and a web-based reporting layer to demonstrate a complete FP&A analytics workflow. The objective is not simply to visualize the source data, but to transform it into auditable management measures.
